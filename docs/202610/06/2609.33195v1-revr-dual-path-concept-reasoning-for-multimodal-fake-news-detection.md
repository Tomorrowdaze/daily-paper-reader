## 摘要
视觉-语言模型（VLM）通过生成显式分析来支持多模态虚假新闻检测（FND）。近期方法通过将核验知识组织为显式概念进一步提升可解释性。然而，仍有两个问题：如何提升核验概念的可靠性与适用性，以及如何高效地运用可复用的概念去核验未见过的新闻。我们提出 ReVR，这一双路径推理框架，用于为多模态虚假新闻检测构建并应用可复用的核验概念。智能体式工作流对候选概念进行锚定并加以整合，而统计画像则刻画其历史行为。在推理阶段，一个面向覆盖性的路径通过可训练的编码器从完整概念库中聚合证据；与此同时，一个面向查询的路径则提示冻结的 VLM 在选定的概念及其观测之上进行推理。当两条路径的预测不一致时，一个经学习的冲突解决器在二者之间进行选择。在虚假新闻基准上的实验表明，该方法在检测性能与泛化性方面具有有效性。

## Abstract
Vision-language models (VLMs) support multimodal fake news detection (FND) by producing explicit analyses. Recent methods further improve interpretability by organizing verification knowledge into explicit concepts. However, two questions remain: how to improve the reliability and applicability of verification concepts, and how to effectively apply reusable concepts to verify unseen news. We propose \textbf{ReVR}, a dual-path reasoning framework that constructs and applies reusable verification concepts for multimodal fake news detection. An agentic workflow grounds and consolidates candidate concepts, while statistical profiles characterize their historical behavior. During inference, a coverage-oriented path aggregates evidence from the complete concept library using a trainable encoder, while a query-focused path prompts a frozen VLM to reason over selected concepts and their observations. A learned conflict resolver selects between the two predictions when they disagree. Experiments on fake news benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of the method regarding detection performance and generalizability.
