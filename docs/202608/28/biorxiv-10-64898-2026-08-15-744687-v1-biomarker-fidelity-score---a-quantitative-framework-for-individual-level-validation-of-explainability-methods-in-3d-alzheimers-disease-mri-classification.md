## 摘要
应用于阿尔茨海默病神经影像深度学习模型的可解释性方法会产生归因图，而不同方法与架构之间差异显著；然而，目前尚无经过验证的定量框架，可用于在个体受试者层面判定哪种方法能够最忠实地将归因信号定位于既定的AD生物标志物解剖区域。现有验证方法依赖群体层面的比较或定性的可视化检查，从而未能表征个体层面的生物标志物对齐。
我们提出生物标志物保真度评分（Biomarker Fidelity Score, BFS），这是一种定量工具，用于衡量个体层面三维可解释性归因图与经图谱配准的、与AD相关的神经影像ROI（感兴趣区）之间的空间重叠，覆盖十三个解剖学定义的结构，包括海马、内嗅皮层、杏仁核和海马旁回。我们在327名类别均衡的ADNI-3受试者上，跨三种三维体积网络架构（3D ResNet-18、DenseNet-121、Swin-UNETR）对五种可解释性方法（GradCAM++、Integrated Gradients、DeepSHAP、LRP、ScoreCAM）进行了基准评测。Integrated Gradients在所有架构上均取得最高的BFS，而GradCAM++始终表现出最低的生物标志物对齐度（Friedman检验，均p<0.001）。在207名独立的OASIS-3受试者上，无需重新训练，完整的BFS流程复现了上述排名；在全部十五种方法-架构组合中，最大绝对差异为0.0005，且跨队列排名的Spearman秩相关系数为0.964。通过提供一个经外部验证、基于生物标志物、面向个体层面的定量标准，BFS为临床医生和AI开发者在AD神经影像中选择值得信赖的可解释性方法提供了切实可行的指导。

## Abstract
Explainability methods applied to deep learning models for Alzheimer's disease neuroimaging produce attribution maps that vary substantially across methods and architectures, yet no validated quantitative framework exists for determining which method most faithfully localises attribution signal within established AD biomarker anatomy at the individual subject level. Existing validation approaches rely on group-level comparisons or qualitative visual inspection, leaving individual-level biomarker alignment uncharacterised. We introduce the Biomarker Fidelity Score (BFS), a quantitative tool measuring spatial overlap between individual-level 3D explainability attention maps and atlas-registered AD-relevant neuroimaging ROIs across thirteen anatomically defined structures including hippocampus, entorhinal cortex, amygdala, and parahippocampal gyrus. Five explainability methods (GradCAM++, Integrated Gradients, DeepSHAP, LRP, ScoreCAM) were benchmarked across three volumetric architectures (3D ResNet-18, DenseNet-121, Swin-UNETR) on 327 balanced ADNI-3 subjects. Integrated Gradients achieved the highest BFS across all architectures while GradCAM++ consistently showed the lowest biomarker alignment (all p<0.001, Friedman test). The complete BFS pipeline replicated these rankings without retraining on 207 independent OASIS-3 subjects, with maximum absolute difference of 0.0005 across all fifteen method-architecture combinations and Spearman rank correlation of 0.964 between cohort rankings. By offering an externally validated, individual-level, biomarker-grounded quantitative standard, BFS equips clinicians and AI developers with practical guidance for selecting trustworthy explainability methods in AD neuroimaging.
