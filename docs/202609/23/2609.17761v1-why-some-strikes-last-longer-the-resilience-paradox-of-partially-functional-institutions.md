## 摘要
劳资罢工并不必然导致机构完全瘫痪，尤其是在未参与罢工的部门维持部分正常活动时。我们提出一个最简动力学模型，以研究此类残余活动如何影响劳资冲突的持续性。该模型通过两种相互竞争的机制耦合罢工参与度、机构响应与整体功能水平：其一是残余活动对罢工参与度的直接抑制效应，其二是由于解决冲突的制度压力下降而引发的间接效应。解析计算揭示了一个反直觉的情形：增加残余活动会提高而非降低稳态罢工参与度。我们通过罢工响应敏感度来表征这一行为，并推导出一个精确的补偿阈值，将常规情形与“韧性悖论”情形分隔开。在该阈值处，两种机制恰好平衡，使稳态罢工参与度与残余活动无关。数值结果验证了分析预测并阐明了其背后的机制。我们的结果表明，机构韧性可能产生意想不到的动力学后果：通过吸收部分罢工造成的扰动，持续运转可能降低机构响应的紧迫性，从而促进冲突的持续。这提供了一个简单的例证，说明在复杂社会系统中，单个具有稳定作用的过程在整体上可能共同产生反直觉的结果。

## Abstract
Labor strikes do not necessarily lead to complete institutional disruption, particularly when non-striking sectors maintain part of normal activities. We propose a minimal dynamical model to investigate how such residual activity affects the persistence of labor conflicts. The model couples strike participation, institutional response, and overall functionality through two competing mechanisms: a direct suppressive effect of residual activity on strike participation and an indirect effect arising from the reduced institutional pressure to resolve the conflict. Analytical calculations reveal a counterintuitive regime in which increasing residual activity increases, rather than decreases, stationary strike participation. We characterize this behavior through a strike-response susceptibility and derive an exact compensation threshold separating the conventional regime from a resilience-paradox regime. At the threshold, the two mechanisms exactly balance, making stationary strike participation independent of residual activity. Numerical results confirm the analytical predictions and illustrate the underlying mechanism. Our results show that institutional resilience can have an unintended dynamical consequence: by absorbing part of the disruption generated by a strike, continued operation may reduce the urgency of institutional response and thereby favor conflict persistence. This provides a simple example of how individually stabilizing processes can collectively generate counterintuitive outcomes in complex social systems.
