## 摘要
中东各国的冲突事件呈现出复杂的时间波动。作为一个简单的基线，我们使用自回归积分移动平均（ARIMA）框架检验其冲突频率时间序列是否包含可度量的线性时间结构，该框架假设当前值对过去观测与误差存在线性依赖。尽管这一线性假设较为简单，ARIMA在大多数国家中仍能在很大程度上刻画时间依赖性。基于所选ARIMA模型的预测在一定程度上再现了总体时间行为，但突发变化仍难以预测。我们进一步使用第一个过零点k*（表征初始正时间相关性的持续时长）以及积分相关时间τ_int（进一步反映其强度与衰减）来考察冲突活动变化的短期持续性。结果显示，k*约为2—6天，而τ_int约为0.6至1.7天。τ_int与人均GDP的平均水平之间存在显著负相关，这暗示短期冲突持续性可能与更广泛的社会经济条件相关。上述结果揭示了可度量的线性时间结构以及因国而异的短期持续性，存在于中东地区冲突活动的时间模式之中。

## Abstract
Conflict events in Middle Eastern countries exhibit complex temporal fluctuations. As a simple baseline, we examine whether their conflict-frequency time series contain measurable linear temporal structure using the autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA) framework, which assumes linear dependence on past observations and errors. Despite this simple linear assumption, the temporal dependence is largely captured for most countries by ARIMA. Predictions based on the selected ARIMA models reproduce the overall temporal behavior to some extent, although abrupt changes remain difficult to predict. We further examine the short-term persistence of changes in conflict activity using the first zero-crossing point $k^*$, which characterizes the duration of the initial positive temporal correlation, and the integrated correlation time $τ_{\mathrm{int}}$, which additionally reflects its strength and decay. The resulting $k^*$ values are approximately 2--6 days, while $τ_{\mathrm{int}}$ ranges from approximately 0.6 to 1.7 days. The significant negative association of $τ_{\mathrm{int}}$ with mean GDP per capita suggests a possible relation between short-term conflict persistence and broader socioeconomic conditions. These results reveal measurable linear temporal structure and country-dependent short-term persistence in the temporal patterns of conflict activity across the Middle East.
